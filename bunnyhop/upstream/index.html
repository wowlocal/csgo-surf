bunnyhop.lol: Counter-Strike: Source bunnyhop in your browser

Classic CS:S bhop, straight in the browser. The real Source movement at 100 tick on the maps you grew up on, from bhop_eazy to the Prestige-era classics. No install, no download, no account needed.

  • Hundreds of classic bhop maps, tiered T1 (very easy) to T6 (extreme).
  • Your PB ghost races you on every attempt; race the WR or anyone's run.
  • Tick-exact replays with a full viewer, cameras and MP4 export.
  • Verified leaderboards: every run is re-simulated on the server before it counts.
  • Styles: Normal, Legit Scroll, W-Only, Sideways, Half-Sideways.
  • Online servers with map votes, RTV, nominations and chat.
